How long is a class?

The breakfast class takes 2 hours 30 minutes. Lunch and dinner take about 3 hours. Every class includes sitting down to eat the meal you cooked.

How many people are in a class?

A class goes ahead once 3 guests have booked, and takes up to 16. You can book a single space for yourself, and we'll let you know when your class is confirmed or help you move to another date.

Do I need cooking experience?

No. Melisha teaches every step, so you can start from zero.

What's included?

The full meal you cook, drinks as listed for your class, and a recipe card to take home.

How do I book and pay?

Send a booking request. We'll confirm your date by email or WhatsApp and tell you how to pay.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel at least 48 hours before your class for a full refund.

What about allergies?

Tell us when you book. Our classes use shrimp, chicken, seafood (in the ceviche), wheat and dairy.

Can we book a private class or a big group?

Yes. A private class for two, or a special rate for groups of ten or more.

Where are you?

On the corner of Avenida Mangle and Luciano Reyes Street, Caye Caulker.
